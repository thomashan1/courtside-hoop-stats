# Privacy Policy — Courtside Hoop Stats

_Last updated: July 21, 2026_

Courtside Hoop Stats ("the app") is a basketball statistics tracker. This policy
explains what information the app handles. In short: **the app does not collect,
transmit, or share any personal data.**

## Information we collect

**None.** We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information.

## Information you enter

You can enter data into the app — such as player names, jersey numbers, game
details, scores, and notes. **This information is stored only on your device.**
It is never sent to the developer, to any server, or to any third party. If you
delete the app, this data is removed with it.

## Analytics and tracking

The app contains **no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking**
software. We do not use cookies or device identifiers for tracking.

## Children's privacy

The app may be used to record statistics for youth sports, which can include
minors' names and jersey numbers. Because all such information stays on your
device and is never collected by us or transmitted anywhere, we do not knowingly
collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Data storage and security

All app data is stored locally on your device using the operating system's
standard storage. Its security is governed by your device's own protections
(passcode, Face ID/Touch ID, and encryption).

## Changes to this policy

If the app adds features that handle data differently — for example, optional
iCloud sync to share stats across devices — this policy will be updated before
those features are released, and this page will show a new "Last updated" date.
