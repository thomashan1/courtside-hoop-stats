# Privacy Policy — Courtside Hoop Stats

_Last updated: August 5, 2026_

Courtside Hoop Stats ("the app") is a basketball statistics tracker. This policy
explains what information the app handles. In short: **the developer does not
collect, transmit, or sell any personal data.** Everything you enter stays on
your device unless you choose to share a team, in which case it goes through
**your own iCloud account** to the people you invite — and nowhere else.

## Information we collect

**None.** We do not collect, receive, sell, or share any personal information.
The app has no accounts, no analytics, and no server of ours.

## Information you enter

You can enter data into the app — player names, jersey numbers, game details,
scores, and notes. **This is stored on your device.** It is never sent to the
developer or to any third party. Deleting the app removes it.

## Sharing a team (optional)

If you choose to share a team, that team's roster and games are copied to
**your own iCloud account** using Apple's CloudKit, so that the people you invite
can see them. This is the same mechanism behind an iCloud Shared Album.

- **It only happens if you start it.** No data leaves your device until you share
  a team, and stopping sharing removes the shared copy.
- **Only people you invite** can see it. Invitations go to specific Apple
  Accounts; there is no public link.
- **They can view, not change.** People you invite see the roster, scores, and
  stats read-only.
- **The developer cannot see any of it.** The data sits in your iCloud account,
  governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
  We have no access to it and receive no copy.

Because a shared team's roster may include **minors' names and jersey numbers**,
share only with people you intend to give that information to — the same care you
would take sending a team photo.

## Notifications

If you follow a team someone shared with you, the app can notify you about game
activity. Notifications are generated **on your device** from data already synced
through your iCloud account; no notification content is sent to the developer.
You can change the frequency, or turn notifications off entirely, in Settings.

## Analytics and tracking

The app contains **no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking**
software. We do not use cookies or device identifiers for tracking.

## Children's privacy

The app may be used to record statistics for youth sports, which can include
minors' names and jersey numbers. That information stays on your device, or —
if you share a team — within your own iCloud account and the accounts of people
you invite. It is never collected by us, so we do not knowingly collect personal
information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Data storage and security

App data is stored on your device using the operating system's standard storage,
protected by your device's own passcode, Face ID/Touch ID, and encryption. Data
for a shared team is additionally stored in your iCloud account and secured by
Apple.

## Changes to this policy

If the app adds features that handle data differently, this policy will be
updated before those features are released, and this page will show a new
"Last updated" date.
