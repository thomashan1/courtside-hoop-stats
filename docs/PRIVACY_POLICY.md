# Privacy Policy — Courtside Hoop Stats

_Last updated: September 18, 2026_

Courtside Hoop Stats ("the app") is a basketball statistics tracker. This policy
explains what information the app handles. In short: **the developer does not
collect, transmit, or sell any personal data.** Everything you enter stays on
your device, and on **your own iCloud account** — where it is backed up for you,
and where it goes to the people you invite if you share a team. It goes nowhere
else.

## Information we collect

**None.** We do not collect, receive, sell, or share any personal information.
The app has no accounts, no analytics, and no server of ours.

## Information you enter

You can enter data into the app — player names, jersey numbers, game details,
scores, and notes. **This is stored on your device**, and backed up to your own
iCloud account (below). It is never sent to the developer. Deleting the app
removes it from the device.

## iCloud backup

So that a lost, broken or replaced phone doesn't take the season with it, the
app copies your teams and games to **your own iCloud account** (Apple's
CloudKit, private database) as you record them. This is the same iCloud account
the rest of your device uses; no account is created and nothing is sent to the
developer.

- **It is your copy, not ours.** The data sits in your iCloud, governed by
  [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). The developer
  has no access to it and receives no copy.
- **Nobody else can see it.** A backup is private to your account — it is not
  shared with anyone, including people who follow one of your teams.
- **Restoring only adds.** Bringing a team or game back from iCloud never
  overwrites or deletes what's already on the phone.
- **Deleting a game deletes its backup.** The backup follows what you actually
  have rather than keeping copies you thought were gone.

Settings shows when the last backup ran and what is in iCloud.

## Sharing a team (optional)

If you choose to share a team, that team's roster and games are copied to
**your own iCloud account** using Apple's CloudKit, so that the people you invite
can see them. This is the same mechanism behind an iCloud Shared Album.

- **It only happens if you start it.** Nobody else can see a team until you
  share it, and stopping sharing removes the shared copy.
- **Only people you invite** can see it. Invitations go to specific Apple
  Accounts; there is no public link.
- **They can view, not change.** People you invite see the roster, scores,
  stats, and any notes you write on a game — read-only.
- **The developer cannot see any of it.** The data sits in your iCloud account,
  governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
  We have no access to it and receive no copy.

Because a shared team's roster may include **minors' names and jersey numbers**,
share only with people you intend to give that information to — the same care you
would take sending a team photo.

## Notifications

If you follow a team someone shared with you, the app can notify you about game
activity. Notifications are generated **on your device** from data already synced
through your iCloud account; no notification content is sent to the developer.
You can change the frequency, or turn notifications off entirely, in Settings.

## Analytics and tracking

The app contains **no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking**
software. We do not use cookies or device identifiers for tracking.

## Children's privacy

The app may be used to record statistics for youth sports, which can include
minors' names and jersey numbers. That information stays on your device and in
your own iCloud account — and, if you share a team, in the accounts of the
people you invite. It is never collected by us, so we do not knowingly collect personal
information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Data storage and security

App data is stored on your device using the operating system's standard storage,
protected by your device's own passcode, Face ID/Touch ID, and encryption. Your
backup — and a shared team's data — is additionally stored in your iCloud
account and secured by Apple.

## Changes to this policy

If the app adds features that handle data differently, this policy will be
updated before those features are released, and this page will show a new
"Last updated" date.
